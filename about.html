About the Trust

Our story, mission, and guiding light.

Love All, Serve All

Sathya Sai Prema Kuterram is a spiritual trust established to propagate the universal teachings of Sri Sathya Sai Baba. Rooted in the core principles of Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, and Non-Violence, the trust serves as a beacon of spiritual light and selfless service.

Our name — Prema Kuterram — means "Abode of Love." We strive to be exactly that: a home where love guides every action, every service, and every relationship.

Through regular bhajans, study circles, seva activities, and community programs, we bring together devotees and seekers from all walks of life, fostering a community built on compassion, mutual respect, and devotion.

Our Core Values

The five human values that guide everything we do.

💡

Truth Sathya

Living and speaking truth in thought, word, and deed.

✌️

Right Conduct Dharma

Acting with integrity, responsibility, and righteousness.

☞️

Peace Shanti

Cultivating inner calm and harmony in all relationships.

❤️

Love Prema

Unconditional love for all beings as expressions of the divine.

🕊️

Non-Violence Ahimsa

Compassion and harmlessness in thought, word, and action.
