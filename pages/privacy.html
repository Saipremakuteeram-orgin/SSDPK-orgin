Privacy Policy

Sri Sai Dharma Samrakshana Prema Kuteeram ("SSPK", "we") values your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Registration details you provide on signup (name, email, phone, language).
  • Seva/donation records processed securely via Razorpay.
  • Basic usage analytics via Vercel Analytics.

How we use it

We use your information to operate the member portal, send welcome and event emails, process seva contributions, and improve the website. We do not sell your personal data.

Data retention & contact

You may request access or deletion of your data by emailing info@sathyasaipremakuterram.org.
